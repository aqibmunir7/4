Our story

A neighbourhood cafe, run by Imogen & Louie

Albatross is run by Imogen & Louie — a speciality-coffee cafe and micro bakery on North Street, Southville.

It is the kind of place people come back to: sunlit, laid-back, with cosy corners to stay for hours over a coffee and a pastry. Dogs are welcome, and the drinks list changes with what is pouring.

If you have only seen Albatross on Instagram, this page is the missing piece: the address, the hours, and what to order when you get there.
